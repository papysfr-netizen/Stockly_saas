## English

**App name** (30 characters max)

```
Nguli – Cash, Stock, Credit
```

**Short description** (80 characters max)

```
Sales, cash, stock and credit for your shop, in $ and Congolese francs, offline
```

**Full description** (4,000 characters max)

```
Nguli, "the granary" in the Great Lakes languages, keeps track of all your shop's money: what you sell, what is in the cash drawer, what customers owe you and what you owe.

Made in Goma for shops, drink depots, pharmacies, hardware and grocery stores in Congo.

💵 SALES AND CASH REGISTER
• Fast sales, with search or barcode scanning
• Payment in dollars and Congolese francs, even mixed: Nguli computes the change
• Cash or Mobile Money
• No small change? Put it on the customer's account
• Printed receipt or sent on WhatsApp

🔒 CASH CLOSING
• Count the drawer at the end of the day without seeing the expected amount
• Nguli shows at once if money is missing

📦 STOCK
• Stock in, stock out and transfers between your shops
• Import products from Excel or from a simple photo of your list
• Different price per shop

📒 CREDIT AND DEBTS
• Who owes you and whom you owe: credit sales, loans, supplier purchases
• No credit without the customer's name and phone
• WhatsApp reminder in one tap
• Customer accounts: deposits, credits, advances

📊 REPORTS
• Sales, profit, margins and expenses by day, week, month
• Accounting following the SYSCOHADA chart of accounts
• Daily summary to send to the owner on WhatsApp

👥 TEAM
• Owner, manager, cashier: each with their own permissions
• A cashier cannot lower a price or change a sale without your approval, and the server checks it too
• Activity log: who did what
• Suspend or remove an employee at any time

🌍 BUILT FOR HERE
• Works without internet: everything is sent when the connection returns
• French, English, Swahili and Lingala
• Several shops for one owner

Your data stays yours: no ads, no data sold.
```
